The discipline of staying
private until the
work is ready

There is increasing pressure in business to present early, announce quickly, and make unfinished work look complete. In many cases, that pressure is a mistake.

Privacy, when used properly, is not secrecy for its own sake. It is discipline. Credibility is stronger when reality arrives before narrative, not after it.
